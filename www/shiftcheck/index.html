Questions

How is expected pay calculated?

For every shift you logged as worked: paid time (end − start − unpaid break) × hourly rate, plus overtime at your overtime percentage. Daily overtime is counted first; weekly overtime only counts the remaining hours, so no hour is paid as overtime twice. With Pro, night and weekend extras are added. Shifts that are only planned are not counted until you log them.

Why does a cancelled shift still count as planned?

Because it was on your schedule. The summary then shows the hours you lost. Delete a shift if it should not be part of your plan at all.

Will applying a rotation overwrite my changes?

No. Applying a rotation only replaces upcoming rotation shifts you haven't edited or logged. Past shifts and shifts you edited, logged or added yourself are kept.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Shiftcheck. Use Settings › Export Backup to keep a copy in Files or iCloud Drive.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase with no renewal.

What does Delete All Local Data do?

It removes jobs, shifts, rotations, received amounts, drafts and settings from this iPhone. It does not cancel or refund your Pro purchase; you can restore Pro at any time.

Is Shiftcheck payroll software?

No. It helps you check your own records. Your employer's pay statement is the official document.
