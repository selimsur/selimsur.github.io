Shiftcheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Shiftcheck is a personal shift calendar for iPhone. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Shiftcheck has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs. We, the developer, never receive your jobs, shifts, working hours, pay rates, received amounts or any information about how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

  • Your jobs, hourly rates, shift types, rotations, shifts, clock-in times, notes and the amounts you enter as received are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Unsaved edits are kept as drafts on your iPhone so they survive the app being closed.
  • App settings (such as your language choice) are stored on your iPhone.
  • This data is included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings, which are controlled by Apple and you, not by Shiftcheck.
  • Deleting the app removes this data. You can export a backup file from Settings › Export Backup and store it wherever you choose.
  • Settings › Delete All Local Data removes everything Shiftcheck stores on the device. Files you exported elsewhere are not affected.

Permissions

Shiftcheck does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendars, camera, photos or notifications.

Purchases

Shiftcheck Pro is purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro. This is the only time the app connects to the internet.

Sharing

When you export a backup or a CSV file, it is passed to the app or service you choose (for example Files, Mail or Messages). That app's privacy policy applies to what you share.

Children

Shiftcheck does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
