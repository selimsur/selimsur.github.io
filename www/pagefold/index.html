Questions

Where are my PDFs stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Pagefold. Use Settings › Export Backup to keep a copy in Files or iCloud Drive.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase with no renewal.

Why can't I import a PDF?

Password-protected PDFs can't be imported. Remove the password in another app, then import it again.

What does Delete All Local Data do?

It removes PDFs, drafts and settings from this iPhone. It does not cancel or refund your Pro purchase; you can restore Pro at any time.
