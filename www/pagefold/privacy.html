Pagefold Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Pagefold is a PDF maker for iPhone. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Pagefold has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs. We, the developer, never receive your documents, photos, text or any information about how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

  • PDFs you create, drafts, page images and thumbnails are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • App settings (such as your language choice) are stored on your iPhone.
  • This data is included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings, which are controlled by Apple and you, not by Pagefold.
  • Deleting the app removes this data. You can export a backup file from Settings › Export Backup and store it wherever you choose.
  • Settings › Delete All Local Data removes everything Pagefold stores on the device.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document. Scans are processed on your device.
  • Photos are chosen with the system photo picker; Pagefold receives only the photos you select.
  • You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Pagefold Pro is purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro.

Sharing

When you share or export a PDF, it is passed to the app or service you choose (for example Mail, Messages or Files). That app's privacy policy applies to what you share.

Children

Pagefold does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
