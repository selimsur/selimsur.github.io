Fotosize Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Fotosize ("the app") makes smaller copies of photos and videos on your iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Summary

  • Fotosize does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.
  • All compression happens on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Photos and videos

  • You choose items with the iOS photo picker. Fotosize can read only the items you pick.
  • Smaller copies are stored inside the app on your iPhone until you delete them. Copies of the originals used during a job are removed when the job is closed.
  • If you tap "Save to Photos", iOS asks for permission to add items to your photo library. Fotosize uses this only to save the copies you choose.
  • Compressed photos are re-encoded without location and camera details (EXIF).
  • If you share a result, it goes to the app or service you pick in the iOS share sheet; that service's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Fotosize Pro is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; Fotosize receives only the confirmation that a purchase is valid, which is checked on your device. We never see your payment details.

Network

The app works offline. It connects to the internet only through Apple's StoreKit to load the Pro price, purchase it and restore it.

Data on your device

App data (results, unfinished job, settings) stays on your iPhone and can be removed at any time with Settings › Delete All Local Data or by deleting the app. Copies you saved to Photos or Files are not affected. Fotosize does not back up your results; if you delete the app, results you have not saved elsewhere are lost.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes
