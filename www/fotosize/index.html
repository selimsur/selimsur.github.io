Questions

Why is my file slightly smaller than the target?

Fotosize always stays at or below the size you choose. It counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes, so the file also passes upload forms that count 1 KB as 1,024 bytes.

The app says the target is too small.

A long video can't stay watchable at a tiny size. Fotosize shows the smallest sensible size; choose that, or trim the video in Photos first.

Are my originals changed?

No. Fotosize always makes a copy. Your originals stay in Photos.

Where are my results?

In Recent on the home screen, only on your iPhone, until you delete them. Save them to Photos or share them to keep a copy elsewhere.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase with no renewal.

What does Delete All Local Data do?

It removes results, any unfinished job and settings from this iPhone. Copies saved to Photos or Files stay. It does not cancel or refund your Pro purchase; you can restore Pro at any time.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
