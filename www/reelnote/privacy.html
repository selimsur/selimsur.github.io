Reelnote Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Reelnote is a watch journal for iPhone. Your journal is stored on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Reelnote has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs. We, the developer, never receive your titles, diary entries, ratings, notes or any information about how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

  • Titles, episodes, diary entries, ratings, notes, lists, posters and drafts are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • App settings (such as your language choice) are stored on your iPhone.
  • This data is included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings, which are controlled by Apple and you, not by Reelnote.
  • Deleting the app removes this data. You can export a backup file from Settings › Export Backup and store it wherever you choose.
  • Settings › Delete All Local Data removes everything Reelnote stores on the device.

Series catalog (TVmaze)

When you search for a series, Reelnote sends your search text directly from your iPhone to the public TVmaze API (api.tvmaze.com). When you add a series, or when Reelnote refreshes the episodes of a series you are watching, it sends that series' TVmaze ID. Posters are downloaded from TVmaze's image server and then stored on your device. As with any internet request, TVmaze receives your device's IP address. Reelnote sends no other information, and nothing from your journal. TVmaze's own privacy policy (on tvmaze.com) applies to those requests. Series data is licensed under CC BY-SA by TVmaze.

Movie catalog (TMDB)

When you search for a movie, Reelnote sends your search text and your app language directly from your iPhone to The Movie Database API (api.themoviedb.org). When you add a movie, and when Reelnote refreshes its details (TMDB allows its data to be kept for at most six months, so details and posters of added movies are refreshed about every five months while you are online), it sends that movie's TMDB ID. Posters are downloaded from TMDB's image server (image.tmdb.org) and stored on your device. TMDB receives your device's IP address and the app's access token, which identifies the app, not you. Reelnote sends no other information, and nothing from your journal. TMDB's own privacy policy (on themoviedb.org) applies to those requests. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Photos

If you choose a poster for a title you add yourself, the system photo picker is used. Reelnote receives only the photo you select and stores a copy on your device.

Purchases

Reelnote Pro is purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro.

Sharing

Backups and CSV exports are created only when you ask for them and are shared through the iOS share sheet to a destination you choose.

Children

Reelnote does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
