Reelnote Support

Reelnote is a private watch journal for TV series and movies. Your journal stays on your iPhone.

Questions

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Reelnote. Use Settings › Export Backup to keep a copy in Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Export a backup on the old iPhone, then use Settings › Restore from Backup on the new one.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase with no renewal.

A series isn't in the search results.

Use “Add Manually” and enter the number of seasons and episodes yourself.

New episodes are missing.

Open the series, tap ••• › Refresh Episodes (needs internet). Reelnote also refreshes the series you watch once a day when you open the app.

What does Delete All Local Data do?

It removes your titles, diary, lists, posters and settings from this iPhone. It does not cancel or refund your Pro purchase; you can restore Pro at any time.
